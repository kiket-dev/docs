---
title: Build your first monitored process
description: Author a contract-review process in the Modeling Cockpit, simulate before merge, and ship file-backed YAML in about 15 minutes.
---

By the end of this walkthrough you'll have a three-state monitored process with an SLA, a two-person approval chain, and an automation that posts to Slack on breach. Every step is doable in the **Modeling Cockpit** — we'll also show the YAML so you can see what the editor writes.

<Callout>
Prefer YAML-first? Paste the workflow at the bottom of this page into `.kiket/workflows/contract_review.yaml` in your workspace configuration repository and open it in the Modeling Cockpit to verify structure and run simulation.
</Callout>

## Goal: a contract-review process

You need legal to review every contract, CFO to co-sign anything over $100k, and the team to be paged if review drags past 48 hours.

<Steps>

<Step>

### Open the Modeling Cockpit

From the workspace sidebar, open **Processes** and click **New process** (or open an existing process and choose **Edit in Modeling Cockpit**). Enter a key (`contract_review`), a name (`Contract Review`), and confirm.

You'll land in the Modeling Cockpit with an empty canvas and a single **Intake** state pre-dropped. The editor opens `.kiket/workflows/contract_review.yaml` in your linked configuration repository.

</Step>

<Step>

### Add the Legal Review state

Click **+ Add state** in the toolbar. Name it **Legal Review**. The canvas places it to the right of Intake; drag it wherever you like.

Click the state to open its inspector. In the **Category** dropdown, leave it as *Active*.

</Step>

<Step>

### Connect Intake → Legal Review

Hover Intake; a small arrow appears on its right edge. Drag from the arrow to the Legal Review state. A transition is created. Click the transition itself to name it — `Start review`.

</Step>

<Step>

### Add the SLA

Back in the inspector for Legal Review, scroll to **SLA**. Set:

- **Warning**: `24h` (notify team early)
- **Breach**: `48h` (escalate to on-call)

Save the file. Autosave commits YAML to your workspace repository; you'll see a brief save indicator in the header.

<Callout title="What just happened?">
The Modeling Cockpit wrote `.kiket/workflows/contract_review.yaml` in your configuration repository. Open the repo on GitHub to inspect the diff — canvas and YAML are the same source of truth.
</Callout>

</Step>

<Step>

### Add the approval chain

Still in the Legal Review inspector, scroll to **Approval**. Click **Add approver**. Pick `counsel` (the counsel group in your org). Add another approver: `cfo`. Leave the type as *Sequential*.

Now *any* transition out of Legal Review requires counsel → CFO sign-off in order.

</Step>

<Step>

### Add the Approved state

Click **+ Add state**. Name it **Approved**. In the inspector, set **Category** to *Final*. Drag a transition from Legal Review to Approved and name it `Approve`.

</Step>

<Step>

### Wire the breach automation

On the Legal Review state, open the inspector's **Automations** tab. Click **Add automation** and pick the trigger `on_sla_breach`. For the action, pick **Send notification** and choose your Slack channel from the dropdown (if you haven't connected Slack yet, do that first — see [Your first extension](/docs/start/first-extension)).

Save. Now when a case sits in Legal Review past 48h, Slack gets a message.

</Step>

<Step>

### Simulate before merge

Open **Check change** in the Modeling Cockpit toolbar (or add `?simulate=1` to the editor URL). The **Impact** tab forecasts guard blockers; use **Cases** and **History** to replay open cases against your edits.

Click **Re-run** after each change. You'll see:

- Green check when a transition is allowed.
- Red cross + reason when it's blocked (missing approval, SLA not expired, missing evidence, etc.).

Simulation is read-only against live case data using the **saved** workflow file — unsaved buffer edits preview until you save. Resolve blockers on the **Ship** tab before merging process changes to production.

</Step>

<Step>

### Ship the process

When the **Ship** tab shows ready, save (if needed) and merge your configuration change through your normal git workflow. The monitored process becomes active for the workspace: operators can open cases against it, evidence adapters can link proof, and scanner runs evaluate compliance.

Your repo's `.kiket/workflows/contract_review.yaml` now contains everything you configured in the Modeling Cockpit.

</Step>

</Steps>

## The YAML the Modeling Cockpit wrote

```yaml
key: contract_review
name: Contract Review
states:
  intake:
    category: initial

  legal_review:
    category: active
    sla:
      warning: 24h
      breach: 48h
    approval:
      type: sequential
      chain: [counsel, cfo]
    on_sla_breach:
      - notify:
          channel: slack:#contracts

  approved:
    category: final

transitions:
  - from: intake
    to: legal_review
    name: Start review
  - from: legal_review
    to: approved
    name: Approve
    approval_required: true
```
