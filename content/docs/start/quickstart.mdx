You'll create an organization, choose a **monitored process** template, connect or seed an **evidence source**, run a first scan, review findings, and generate an audit-ready snapshot. No credit card is required.

<Callout type="info">
A workspace is the tenant-local home for process configuration and runtime evidence. The configuration remains file-backed under `.kiket/` (include `workspace.yaml` for repository metadata alongside workflows and templates), so changes are versioned and reviewable, but the app guides the setup without asking first-time users to understand git.
</Callout>

## Walkthrough

<Steps>

<Step>

### Create your account

Visit [app.kiket.dev](https://app.kiket.dev/register) and sign up with **email and password** or with **Google** or **GitHub**. You'll land in an empty organization — its name is taken from your email domain, you can rename it later. For details on linking accounts and sign-in options, see [Sign-in and accounts](/docs/start/sign-in-and-accounts).

</Step>

<Step>

### Pick the first outcome

In onboarding, choose the outcome you want to monitor first. Kiket shows what the template creates before you commit:

- process states and case types
- expected evidence
- likely scanner findings
- evidence sources to connect
- report output

For the fastest path, choose **Explore sample data** or a template with a built-in evidence path such as production change approval.

</Step>

<Step>

### Create your workspace and first process

The **same guided flow** creates the organization (if needed), the workspace, and the first monitored process—template, new config repository, or existing `.kiket/` connection. You can rename the organization and workspace later.

When you finish, Kiket records a **process** linked to that workspace. That completes **foundation** setup; you are not asked to “pick a template again” on the dashboard unless the product has a workspace but no process yet (then **Processes** is the right next step, not full onboarding).

</Step>

<Step>

### Activation checklist on Process Twin

After foundation is in place, the Process Twin dashboard shows an **activation** checklist—not a second onboarding wizard. Headings change by state:

- **Get started** — no workspace yet; the primary action points back to full setup.
- **Finish modeling** — workspace exists without a monitored process; the UI sends you to **Processes**.
- **Activate your operational twin** — workspace and process exist; the checklist focuses on evidence, cases, scans, findings, and audit output.

Each step still deep-links to the right screen and names the output it should create:

- raw events and normalized evidence
- the first monitored operational case
- a scanner run with explainable output
- a remediation trail or resolved gap
- an audit snapshot with integrity proof context

If you are evaluating Kiket, use this checklist as the acceptance path after setup: every completed item should leave visible evidence in the product.

</Step>

<Step>

### Connect an evidence source

Open **Settings → Evidence Sources** and connect the source that can prove the process happened. Typical first sources are:

- GitHub for pull requests, reviews, commits, and deployment evidence
- Jira or Linear for work item status and ownership
- Slack or Teams for approval and incident context
- webhook/API ingestion for internal systems

Each source produces raw events, normalized operational events, evidence records, and scanner context.

</Step>

<Step>

### Run the first scan

After one case and at least one evidence record exist, run or wait for the first scanner pass. The scanner compares the process model with observed reality and creates findings such as:

- missing approval evidence
- evidence source disconnected
- stale access review
- SLA risk or breach

</Step>

<Step>

### Resolve one finding

Open **Findings** to triage the scanner output. The inbox groups actionable findings by severity, shows the linked case and evidence count, explains why the finding exists, and recommends the next remediation action. From there, open the case, preview evidence, or start remediation.

### Generate the audit snapshot

Open **Audit Room** and generate a report for the monitored process. The report pulls from the same graph: process model, cases, evidence, findings, remediation, scanner runs, and optional integrity proofs.

</Step>

</Steps>
